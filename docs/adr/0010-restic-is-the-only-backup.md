# restic through stack-back is the only Backup

Copies made "just in case" (`.bak` files, dated dumps, copies of certificate stores) pile up, hold sensitive data in a second place, and are never removed. The stack-back restic repository, stored offsite, is the only Backup. stack-back dumps each Stack's databases over the `backup` network ([ADR 0002](0002-role-based-networks-replace-whalewall.md)) and backs up its named volumes nightly. Media on bind mounts is not backed up. An app's own scheduled backups stay as part of its state, not as Backups. Nothing else on the Host is a copy of data.

Before a risky change to a volume, keep a Rollback Volume ([ADR 0009](0009-volumes-named-for-their-stack.md)) instead of an ad-hoc copy, record its release date in the change's PR, and remove it once the change is verified. One kept past its release date is a Stray. A copy that cannot be a Rollback Volume (data that must leave the Host, or a single file) is recorded in its issue or PR with a removal checkbox, and the work is not done until it is ticked.
